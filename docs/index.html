01 / THE IDEA

A little clarification.
A lot more direction.

You do not need a perfect prompt. Start with the idea you have. Deeperas helps your assistant uncover the decisions that matter.

01

Clarify the intent.

One focused question, with options that explain what each choice means. No silent decisions about things that matter to you.

02

Check the evidence.

Your assistant uses its available files, tools, and sources to investigate facts, rather than asking you to supply what it can find.

03

Deliver the work.

Once the relevant choices are settled, it answers or acts within your request. If evidence is missing, it says what remains unverified.

✳

Already clear? Just get on with it. A settled task, or a decision you explicitly delegate, does not need an interview. Deeperas targets choices that could change the result.

What does the clarification gate do?

The assistant resolves explicit, unselected alternatives before dependent work. Otherwise, it asks when plausible answers would materially change the result. It can continue independent work whose inputs and method are unaffected by the open choice.

Can I delegate decisions or stop the questions?

Yes. Explicitly delegate the particular choice: “Choose the visual style for me.” You can also say “Answer now with what you can support.” That ends questions for the current cycle; missing targets, evidence, and permissions remain unresolved.

Does it remember project decisions?

In Codex projects, the instructions call for a small Deeperas_Decisions.txt containing confirmed decisions and pending issues. In Git projects, it must first verify that the record is untracked and ignored. When those checks cannot be established, it keeps decisions in the conversation instead. This is not a global memory service.

Are all controls the same in every host?

The core clarification workflow is portable. In ChatGPT, the instructions also define X for supported results without more questions, and List for a reusable prompt after a final answer. Those shorthand controls are not enabled in Codex or Cursor. Use ordinary language there.

What can it verify, and what are its limits?

Verification depends on the files, browsing, and tools your host actually provides. Deeperas does not add those capabilities or guarantee correctness. Model behavior can vary, and instructions do not override your platform's permissions or higher-priority rules.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Once the skill is installed, describe what you want in normal language. You can select an option, answer in your own words, or delegate a decision to your assistant.

Will it ask questions about everything?

Its instructions target meaningful uncertainty and explicit alternatives. Clear, settled requests should proceed directly. For vague creation ideas, it first checks whether you want to begin planning.

Does Deeperas send data to its own service?

The plugin includes instructions and manifests, with no Deeperas backend, MCP server, hooks, or telemetry code. Your AI platform still processes the conversation and may use tools and local files according to its own settings and permissions.

Is it listed in the official marketplaces?

This repository provides direct distribution and host-specific marketplace manifests. It does not imply a listing or approval in the public OpenAI or Cursor directories.
